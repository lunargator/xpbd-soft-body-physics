# Changelog

## 0.1.0

- Surface XPBD solver with distance, shape matching, and volume constraints.
- Unity components for a shared simulation, a soft body, and Unity collider sync.
- Samples are Fish Bowl and Materials.
- Smooth Surface draws a Catmull-Rom skin through the particles. The solver keeps the coarse mesh.
- A static floor pushes a particle back out when that particle sinks in during the step.
